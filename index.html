Momentum Watchlist

Copy symbols to ThinkorSwim:
IN PLAY = your full momentum spec: up/down 10%+, on pace for 5x+ normal volume, still moving intraday, and (when required) a news catalyst. MOVER = a mover that misses one of those. Hit A+ only to see just the IN PLAY names. REL VOL is time-adjusted — "on pace for Nx a normal day," so 5x means something at 9:45am too. SINCE OPEN tells a live mover from a gap-and-die. NEWS shows the latest headline (free-source, best-effort — it can miss or lag, and doesn't prove the news is the cause). Score orders THIS list on THIS refresh — not a prediction. Shares/stop are risk-based suggestions; set your real stop on the chart. Names to STUDY, not signals to buy. Not financial advice.
